# Twenty Nanometres — working plan (Clyde)
Thesis draft: a trigger word is an electrical event; each plate shows the word as the receiving neuron's learned shape (dendrites grown into its letters) and the discharge that crosses the ~20 nm cleft to reach it.
- Medium: Golgi/Cajal silver-stain world. Stains: Golgi Section (photomicrograph, depth of field, amber field), Cajal Plate (pen & ink on aged paper, vermilion annotation), Lantern Slide (negative glass slide).
- Growth: space colonization (Runions 2007) with attractors sampled densely inside glyph outlines (Old Standard TT Bold, OFL), tropism, pipe-model radii, spines, beading for Loss words.
- Spark: dielectric breakdown model (Niemeyer–Pietronero–Wiesmann 1984) from axon bouton to arbor; eta from word arousal; return stroke on the channel that connects; failure for Loss; autapse for Shame.
- Vesicles: stippled cluster in bouton; released count by intensity.
- Word lexicon with register + rough valence/arousal (after Warriner et al. 2013 norms).
